Import rules

  • STR: only FC_REMOVAL rows enter the operational STR register. FC_TRANSFER and FC_REMOVAL-Cancel are excluded.
  • Removal Order Detail and Shipment Detail remain separate sources.
  • Shipment rows retain Amazon tracking number and receive a generated internal shipment ID.
  • Duplicate source rows are flagged; tracking number alone is not a unique key.
  • Product mapping conflicts are sent to exceptions rather than choosing the first mapping.
  • Fee import is Amazon-removal-only and stores actual reported charges.
