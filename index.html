Import rules

  • STR: only FC_REMOVAL rows enter the operational STR register. FC_TRANSFER and FC_REMOVAL-Cancel are excluded. SKU is used as SAP Material Code.
  • Removal Order Detail and Shipment Detail remain separate sources.
  • Shipment rows retain Amazon tracking number and receive a generated internal shipment ID.
  • Duplicate source rows are flagged; tracking number alone is not a unique key.
  • Product Mapping Master: upload the original Excel or CSV file. Unique ASIN/FNSKU/SKU matches are applied automatically; ambiguous mappings are flagged for review.
  • Shipment lines are enriched automatically from available FNSKU, SKU and ASIN identifiers. Conflicting mappings remain flagged and are never guessed.
  • Removal fees are captured from the removal-fee and currency columns in Removal Order Detail; no separate fee report is required.
  • PO tracker: individual transaction / invoice / SKU reference groups are maintained. No forced consolidation based on IRN status.
  • The STR / SAP reference workbook supplies Po Number, Delivery No, Billing Document and MIGO No. Material Description is unavailable in the supplied header and remains blank.
